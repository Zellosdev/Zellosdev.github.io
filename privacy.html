Amelia privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Amelia is a personal assistant used only by the person who built her. She is not available to the public, and nobody else can sign in to her.

Google Calendar

With the owner's permission, Amelia reads events on his Google Calendar and adds events when he asks her to. She uses only the access Google grants for calendar events.

  • Calendar data is used only to answer the owner's own questions and requests.
  • It is processed on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • It is not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.
  • The owner can remove Amelia's access at any time from his Google Account (Security → Third-party apps with account access).

Amelia's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
